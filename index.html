Questions

FAQ

Is Level Up free?

Yes. All four themes, the quest map, focus timer, calendar, and course tracking are free.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your computer. Courses, tasks, calendar events, and progress are saved locally. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

How does the AI assistant work, and where is my API key kept?

The assistant runs on Google's Gemini API, so you bring your own key. Create one in Google AI Studio and paste it into the app.

The key is stored only on your PC, encrypted where your system supports it. It is never shown on the website or sent anywhere except to Google when you use the assistant. When you ask a question, your tasks and calendar are sent to Google so it can answer.

What platforms does it run on?

Windows. Download levelup.exe from the button above.

How do I update?

Level Up has no built-in updater. Download the latest levelup.exe from this page and run it to replace the old version.
